## Editorial decisions and holds

- **Umbrae Noctis Dark Market, November 15:** original event organizers confirm the date and venue but publish no market opening time. The midnight timestamp is **sorting-only**, not event hours. Do not use an unrelated dark-market listing to assert a 2 p.m. start.
- **NoPo Creatives, November 21–22:** original vendor packet gives 10–6 Saturday and 10–5 Sunday. Accessibility note is substantive: **upstairs second-floor lodge hall and no elevator**. Free admission and community programming are stated by the organizer.
- **Plus Plus Bizarre Bazaar, November 21:** original Portland Art Museum event page confirms noon–5, free, in the Fields Ballroom, with indie distributors, zines, film, comics and games.
- **Oddities & Curiosities Expo, November 7–8:** host venue confirms Portland dates and halls; the expo organizer's public FAQ states both days run 10–5. Source confirms ticketed admission, but a price figure is intentionally not copied because ticket options may vary.
- **PDX Moon Market, November 22 and December 19–20:** original organizer homepage lists all three dates and 11–4 hours. The vendor application expressly names **2026**. Original visitor FAQ supports all ages and free entry. This is a compact manually verified seasonal series, **not** an extrapolation into 2027.
- **Portland Flea November Decked Out:** held out despite original organizer mentioning the two-day event because its public pages checked here did **not** confirm its specific 2026 dates.
- No new genre/style claims, artwork rights, crowd/access information or venue facilities were inferred when the original organizers did not state them. The rest of the approved 1,066-event dataset was preserved intact.
